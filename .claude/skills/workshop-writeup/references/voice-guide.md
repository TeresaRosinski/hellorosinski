# Voice guide

Teresa's tone: **warm, confident, direct, clear, kind, capable, empowering.**

Her natural strengths: concrete specifics (places, names, real details), honest reflection about growth, one memorable image. Feedback she's received: her prose can be more succinct and clear.

## Rules
- **Lead with the point.** No throat-clearing openers ("Thank you for…", "I'm excited to…").
- **One idea per sentence.** Short sentences read faster on a phone.
- **Intros answer "what is this, and who did it?" in the first sentence.**
- **No hedges or intensifiers:** cut "I believe", "really", "greatly", "very", "even".
- **Don't say things twice.** If the title, facts line, or another section already says it, cut it.
- **No self-grading adjectives** ("strong demand", "highly engaging"). Let the facts show it.
- **At most one vivid image or quote** that carries a section.
- **Plain language.** A smart reader with no AI or L&D background should follow every sentence. Explain or remove jargon (e.g. "scaffolded" becomes "step by step").
- **Name the company (Epsilon); paraphrase internal leadership quotes.**
- **Her own words win.** When she phrases something well in the interview, use it.
- **Italics** via `*asterisks*` for questions asked in the session, quoted phrases, and theory terms.
