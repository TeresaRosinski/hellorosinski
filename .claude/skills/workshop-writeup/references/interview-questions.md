# Interview questions

Ask **one question per message**. Adapt the wording to what she's already said; skip anything already answered. After each answer, reflect back in a line or two what it adds.

## Setting the scene
1. Which session is this (number and title)? What was it, in one or two sentences?
2. What was the situation before the session? Why was it needed?
3. Whose idea was it? If hers, how did it get approved and scheduled?
4. Who was in the room: roles, levels, how many, live or self-paced, virtual or in person? What was their starting point?
5. When did it happen (month and year), and how long was it?

## What she did
6. Walk me through the session in order. What were the exact questions or prompts, word for word? *(Never write "I opened with…" until this is confirmed.)*
7. What were the learning objectives? What could learners *do* by the end? (2–4, measurable.)
8. What did you design deliberately, and why? (For each choice: what you did, and the reason.)
9. What's your usual practice, and what did you change this time? Why?
10. Did anything in the room change your plan as it happened?

## Evidence
11. What happened as a result? Quotes, requests, follow-ups, later use, anything.
12. How many finished / took part / responded? A rough number or "I don't know" is fine. **Never invent one.**
13. What artifacts exist that can be shared publicly (Mentimeter, slides, tools, screenshots)? When was each made: before, during, or after the session?
13b. Which videos or articles did people watch or read in the session? Get a link to each (check the Mentimeter deck first; see SKILL.md). These go in the `materials` field.

## Reflection
14. If you ran it again, what would you change or add, and why?

## Follow-ups to use whenever an answer is vague
- "How did you know that? Did you observe it, hear it, or assume it?"
- "What were the exact words?"
- "Is that one person's story or a pattern you saw more widely?"
- "Was that intentional at the time, or something you see looking back?"
- "When did that happen: before, during, or after the session?"
- "Did the group decide that, or give input and you decided?"
